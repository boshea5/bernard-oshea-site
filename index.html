About

COMEDY FROM
REAL LIFE.

Bernard O'Shea is an Irish comedian, writer and broadcaster known for finding the ridiculous in everyday family life, middle age and modern Ireland.

Across stand-up, television, radio and social media, the comedy is built around recognition: the moment when an audience thinks, “That is exactly what happens in our house.”

His live work mixes stories, sharp observation, characters and the occasional spectacularly poor life decision.
