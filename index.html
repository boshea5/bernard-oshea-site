BIOG

Bernard O'Shea is an Irish comedian, writer and broadcaster known for finding the funny in ordinary life.

Across stand-up, television, radio and writing, Bernard has built a career around sharp observation, family life and the everyday absurdities of modern Ireland.

He has appeared across Irish television and radio and tours nationally as a live stand-up comedian. His current show, Pick Up, Drop Off, takes on family, fatherhood, getting older and the chaos that happens in between.
