About

COMEDY FROM
REAL LIFE.

Bernard O'Shea is a stand-up comedian, broadcaster and bestselling author.

His debut book, My Wife is Married to a Feckin’ Eejit, became a bestseller. A familiar voice on Irish radio, Bernard co-hosted Breakfast Republic on RTÉ 2FM and iRadio Breakfast, and has contributed regularly to RTÉ Radio 1.

On television, Bernard co-wrote and starred in RTÉ's The Republic of Telly and Bridget & Eamon, as well as Marty and Bernard: On the Road Again and Bernard's Working Comics.
