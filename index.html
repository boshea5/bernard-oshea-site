ABOUT BERNARD

COMEDY BUILT FROM REAL LIFE.

Bernard O'Shea is an Irish stand-up comedian, writer and broadcaster. His work turns family life, getting older and everyday Irish absurdity into sharply observed comedy.

He has worked across television, radio and live performance, and tours Ireland with his latest stand-up show Pick Up, Drop Off.
