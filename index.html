About

COMEDY FROM
REAL LIFE.

Bernard O'Shea is an Irish comedian, writer and broadcaster known for finding the ridiculous in everyday family life, middle age, modern Ireland and the small things that somehow become very big things.

Across stand-up, television, radio and social media, Bernard's comedy is built around recognition: the moments that make you think, “That is exactly what happens in our house.”

His live work combines sharp observation, stories, characters and the occasional spectacularly poor life decision.
